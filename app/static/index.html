05 / IDENTIFICATION & LIMITS

What V1 does not claim

01

No verified trading signal. A fare response function has not been estimated or validated against airline or stock outcomes.

02

Spot is not paid fuel. EIA Gulf Coast spot is a public cost-pressure proxy, not United's actual daily delivered fuel expense.

03

Quotes are not yield. Offered fares cannot automatically stand in for realized PRASM, and route/booking composition matters.

04

No look-ahead backtest. Historic observation dates are not verified publication vintages. Daily investment validation is a later deliverable.
