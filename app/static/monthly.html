Are the numerical results stable?

Repeat the saved scenario with independent seeds and increasing path counts. Check sampling variation in both pricing modes at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, plus your chosen endpoint. This measures Monte Carlo error conditional on the current assumptions; forecast accuracy and model uncertainty require separate evidence.

Sampling budget and stopping tolerances

Adds counts up to 1,000,000 and uses 10,000-path batches. Your tolerances and seed-repeat count stay as entered. Extended checks can take several minutes; exact checkpoint vectors use temporary disk space.

Defaults: 12 repeats; monetary tolerance is the larger of $25M and 1% of the replica mean quantile. Margin uses 0.05 percentage points or 1%; cash-floor fractions use 0.5 percentage points. Stop after two consecutive passing counts, at or above the saved run count. The default budget ends at 50,000 paths; you can extend it to 1,000,000. Tolerances are not widened automatically.
